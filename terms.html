The operator is responsible for ensuring that videos processed by AutoShorts are owned by the operator or that the operator has the necessary rights and permissions to upload and publish them.

AutoShorts operates only after explicit Google OAuth authorization. Authorization may be revoked at any time.

The operator is responsible for complying with YouTube's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, copyright requirements, YouTube API Services Terms of Service, and applicable law.

Automation behavior

AutoShorts may read authorized Drive files, derive upload metadata from filenames, upload videos on a schedule, record uploaded file identifiers to prevent duplicates, and add successful uploads to a designated playlist.
